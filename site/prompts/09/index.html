Workshop/Ten prompts/09

Extract actions, dates and unanswered questions

從AI教學指引整理討論清單

Situation

A subject-panel head wants to discuss responsible teacher use of AI and identify which practical questions still need a school decision.

Sources to add to Notebook

the full PDF “Supplement II: Guide to Using AI in Teaching in Primary and Secondary Schools”, linked from the EDB Blueprint page. Add a related circular only if you want it considered and can identify it clearly.

EDB Blueprint & supplement resources ↗

Add the actual publication PDF, not just its landing page. The Blueprint and Supplement II are separate documents.

Use one language version at a time. These are worked examples: adapt the source names to match your notebook.

English promptMarkdown / plain-text prompt

Review the selected ‘Supplement II: Guide to Using AI in Teaching in Primary and Secondary Schools’ for our panel discussion. Focus on teacher preparation and checking AI-generated materials.

Use a six-column table: Source statement; Status (requirement / recommendation / example / aspiration); Applies to; Stated date/condition; Citation; Suggested school response. Preserve ‘may’, ‘should’ and ‘must’; write ‘not specified’ for missing details.

End with three questions for management and one small teacher-preparation pilot. Label proposals as suggestions, not EDB requirements or school decisions.

Write in English.

繁體中文提示詞Markdown / plain-text prompt

請檢視所選AI教學指南（附篇II），供科組討論，集中於教師備課及核對AI教材。

用六欄表格：文件陳述；性質（要求／建議／例子／願景）；適用對象；已列日期／條件；引用；學校回應建議。保留「可」、「應」、「必須」的分別，缺漏資料寫「未有說明」。

最後列三個管理層問題及一項備課小規模試行。提議是建議，不是教育局要求或學校決定。

請以繁體中文回答。

Review [official document/circular] for a Hong Kong secondary-school panel discussion.

Use a six-column table: Source statement; Status (requirement / recommendation / example / aspiration); Applies to; Stated date/condition; Citation; Suggested school response. Keep entries brief. Preserve ‘may’, ‘should’ and ‘must’; write ‘not specified’ for missing details.

End with three questions for management and one small pilot idea. Label proposals as suggestions, not school decisions.

Write in English.

繁體中文提示詞Markdown / plain-text prompt

請檢視[官方文件／通函]，供香港中學科組討論。

用六欄表格：文件陳述；性質（要求／建議／例子／願景）；適用對象；已列日期／條件；引用；學校回應建議。內容簡短，保留「可」、「應」、「必須」的分別，缺漏資料寫「未有說明」。

最後列三個管理層問題及一項小規模試行構思。提議是建議，不是學校決定。
