Compare official documents: updates or contradictions?

比較藍圖及AI教學附篇

Situation

A teacher wants to understand how a broad strategy document relates to a more focused teaching guide, without assuming that either overrides the other.

Sources to add to Notebook

the full Blueprint and the full Supplement II. Give each a distinct source title. Do not use the executive summary as though it were the full Blueprint.

EDB Blueprint & supplement resources ↗English Blueprint PDF ↓Chinese Blueprint PDF ↓

Add the actual publication PDF, not just its landing page. The Blueprint and Supplement II are separate documents.

Use one language version at a time. These are worked examples: adapt the source names to match your notebook.

English promptMarkdown / plain-text prompt

Compare the full ‘Blueprint for Digital Education Development in Primary and Secondary Schools’ with ‘Supplement II: Guide to Using AI in Teaching in Primary and Secondary Schools’. Focus on Hong Kong secondary teachers’ preparation, professional development and checking AI materials.

Use a five-column table: Issue; Blueprint statement and citation; Supplement statement and citation; Relationship/uncertainty; Planning implication. Distinguish scope differences, explicit updates and genuine conflicts. Silence is not a contradiction; do not assume one replaces the other.

End with two short lists: Usable for planning; Needs clarification. Label school proposals as suggestions.

Write in English.

繁體中文提示詞Markdown / plain-text prompt

請比較完整版《中小學數字教育發展藍圖》與所選AI教學指南（附篇II），集中於香港中學教師備課、專業發展及核對AI教材。

用五欄表格：議題；藍圖陳述及引用；附篇陳述及引用；關係／不確定之處；規劃啟示。區分範圍差異、明確更新及真正矛盾。未有提及不等於矛盾，不要假設一份取代另一份。

最後列兩份簡短清單：可用於規劃；需要釐清。學校提議標示為建議。

請以繁體中文回答。
Adapt this task: show the reusable templates

English promptMarkdown / plain-text prompt

Compare [document A] and [document B] on [issue] for Hong Kong secondary schools.

Use a five-column table: Issue; A statement and citation; B statement and citation; Relationship/uncertainty; Planning implication. Distinguish agreement, scope differences, explicit updates and genuine conflicts. Missing coverage is not a contradiction; a newer date alone does not establish replacement.

End with two short lists: Usable for planning; Needs clarification. Label proposed actions as suggestions.

Write in English.

繁體中文提示詞Markdown / plain-text prompt

請從香港中學角度，比較[文件A]與[文件B]對[議題]的說明。

用五欄表格：議題；A陳述及引用；B陳述及引用；關係／不確定之處；規劃啟示。區分共通點、範圍差異、明確更新及真正矛盾。未有提及不等於矛盾，日期較新不等於取代舊文件。

最後列兩份簡短清單：可用於規劃；需要釐清。擬議行動標示為建議。

請以繁體中文回答。
