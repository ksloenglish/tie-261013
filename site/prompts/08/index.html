Workshop/Ten prompts/08

Turn a long EDB document into a staff briefing

為同事整理教育局藍圖摘要

Situation

A subject-panel head has limited meeting time and wants a concise briefing grounded in the actual official publication.

Sources to add to Notebook

the full PDF “Blueprint for Digital Education Development in Primary and Secondary Schools”, obtained from the EDB resource page. If you select only its executive summary, use the alternative sentence included in the prompt.

EDB Blueprint & supplement resources ↗English Blueprint PDF ↓Chinese Blueprint PDF ↓

Add the actual publication PDF, not just its landing page. The Blueprint and Supplement II are separate documents.

Use one language version at a time. These are worked examples: adapt the source names to match your notebook.

English promptMarkdown / plain-text prompt

Brief Hong Kong secondary-school teachers on the selected ‘Blueprint for Digital Education Development in Primary and Secondary Schools’. Start with its purpose and up to three relevant sections with citations.

Use four headings: Main message; Teachers and teaching; School support; Questions to clarify. Use short bullets and keep the briefing within 250 words. Cite key statements.

Distinguish requirements, recommendations and aspirations; label school suggestions separately. State if only the executive summary is selected.

Write in English.

繁體中文提示詞Markdown / plain-text prompt

請根據所選《中小學數字教育發展藍圖》，為香港中學教師整理摘要。先列文件目的及最多三個相關章節，附引用。

用四個標題：主要訊息；教師及教學；學校支援；待釐清問題。用簡短要點，摘要約400字，引用重要陳述。

區分要求、建議及願景，本校回應建議另作標示。如只有行政摘要，請註明。

請以繁體中文回答。

English promptMarkdown / plain-text prompt

Brief Hong Kong secondary-school teachers on the selected [official document]. Start with its purpose and up to three relevant sections with citations.

Then use four headings: Main message; Teachers and teaching; School support; Questions to clarify. Use short bullets and keep the briefing within 250 words. Cite key statements.

Distinguish requirements, recommendations and aspirations; label school suggestions separately. State if only an executive summary is selected.

Write in English.

繁體中文提示詞Markdown / plain-text prompt

請根據所選[官方文件]，為香港中學教師整理摘要。先列文件目的及最多三個相關章節，附引用。

再用四個標題：主要訊息；教師及教學；學校支援；待釐清問題。用簡短要點，摘要約400字，引用重要陳述。

區分要求、建議及願景，本校回應建議另作標示。如只有行政摘要，請註明。

請以繁體中文回答。

Notebook grounds answers in selected sources, but grounding is not an accuracy guarantee. These prompts are workshop adaptations, not official EDB/HKEAA instructions. Check important claims against their cited passages.
