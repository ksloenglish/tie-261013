## Shorter prompts, with the useful details retained｜精簡而具體的提示詞

The prompts specify **task, audience, output structure and language**. Long source registers and repeated “do not invent” lists have been removed. Related follow-up tasks can be requested after checking the first answer.

**Built in:** Notebook grounds its answers in selected sources and provides citations. **Still useful to specify:** the table columns, which sources to compare, how to show missing evidence, and whether new ideas are suggestions rather than official rules. Grounding is not an accuracy guarantee. [4] [16] [17]

The long warning “Do not invent weightings, marks or compulsory question types” is **not mandatory**. For official-guidance tasks, the shorter “Flag anything not stated in the sources” keeps the important instruction without the long list.

**Reusable, not permanently current:** the examples contain no fixed examination year or year placeholder. Before assessment-related work, select the official documents applicable to your cohort and check their versions. Notebook does not automatically replace old imported documents. [1] [16]

**繁體中文：**提示詞保留任務、對象、格式及語言，刪去重複警告及冗長資料清單。Notebook本身會依據所選來源並提供引用，但不保證完全正確。涉及官方要求時，只需簡短指出「資料未有說明的內容，請指出」。實例不綁定考試年份；使用前仍須選取適用於該屆學生的官方文件及核對版本。

## Choose your route and sources

### Route A — General subject preparation

**Create a notebook using existing learning materials for your own teaching subject**, such as lesson notes, worksheets, teacher-authored slides and learning objectives. Choose one topic or unit rather than uploading your entire subject collection. Give the notebook a clear name, for example **“S2 Science — Heat”** or **“S5 Economics — Demand and Supply”**. Add a teacher answer key if relevant and permitted. Use material you are authorised to upload, with no pupil personal data or confidential assessment content. Prepare this notebook before the workshop where possible; otherwise use the opening preparation time.

Then try a prompt that fits your work: **Prompt 3** for lesson preparation, **Prompt 4** for differentiated support, **Prompt 5** for practice questions, or **Prompt 7** for checking a draft. Set the subject, class level and topic to match your notebook. For a non-HKDSE class, replace “HKDSE” and “S4/S5/S6” with your actual course and level; omit official-assessment instructions that do not apply. Ask for source-based classroom practice, not an invented DSE format. You do not need an HKEAA document just to prepare an ordinary lesson or worksheet.

**繁體中文：**請用自己任教科目的現有教材，例如筆記、工作紙、教師自製投影片及學習目標，建立一個聚焦單一課題的筆記本，並清楚命名。再按需要選用教案、不同程度支援、練習或草稿核對提示詞。非HKDSE班別請改成實際課程及年級，刪除不適用的官方評核要求；不要要求AI虛構文憑試格式。教材不得含學生個人資料或機密評核內容。

**Optional HKDSE extension:** If you want to check official curriculum or assessment requirements (especially Prompts 1 and 2), add the applicable Curriculum and Assessment Guide, the assessment framework for the target examination year, and authorised guidance. The [HKEAA framework index](https://www.hkeaa.edu.hk/en/hkdse/assessment/assessment_framework/) lists year-specific changes; the [EDB English curriculum index](https://www.edb.gov.hk/en/curriculum-development/kla/eng-edu/curriculum-documents.html) is one subject example. Use the corresponding sources for your own subject. Official-source-dependent prompts should not be run as though those documents were already in a notes-only notebook. [1] [2]

### Before pasting a prompt

Enter through [the school teacher access page](https://kslo.tech/nb-teachers). Select only relevant sources. Give each a title, version/date, and, for assessment material, target examination year. Replace the `[bracketed fields]`. Choose English or Traditional Chinese and check subject terminology yourself. Click one factual citation and read the supporting passage. Source grounding helps checking; it does not guarantee correctness. [4]

Use permitted, non-sensitive material. **Do not post restricted examination papers, confidential marking material, named pupil work, grades, or private notebook links on Padlet.** Permission to use a document internally is not necessarily permission to upload it to an AI service or redistribute an AI output derived from it. A publicly accessible document is not automatically free of copyright restrictions. Use teacher-authored examples for shareable assessment samples.

## Tell chat how to present the answer｜指定對話答案的呈現方式

**Choose the structure and the length—not just the topic.** Point form is useful for quick briefings; tables for comparing the same fields; numbered steps for procedures and worked solutions. Give column names, heading names and a sensible limit. Google’s prompting guidance recommends explicit response formats and constraints; this is prompting advice, not a guaranteed layout control. [14]

**Markdown is optional—not a required instruction.** For ordinary chat answers, plain-language requests such as “use a four-column table” or “use five short bullet points” are sufficient ways to describe the desired layout. The most useful table instruction gives **both the number of columns and what each column contains**. “Use four columns” alone still leaves Notebook to guess the headings. [14]

| Need | Format instruction to add |
| --- | --- |
| Quick briefing | “Use five bullet points, one short sentence each. Keep citations beside source claims; use fewer points if the evidence supports fewer.” |
| Compare guidance | “Use a four-column table: Issue; Document A; Document B; What needs checking. Keep cells brief and include citations.” |
| A procedure | “Use numbered steps. Put each condition or exception beside its step.” |
| Reusable notes or worksheet | “Use clear headings, numbered questions and a separate Teacher key.” |

**繁體中文用法：**不一定要說「用Markdown」。更實用的指示是：「用四欄表格，欄名為：重點、資料原有意思、對教師的影響、引用出處。」只說欄數仍不夠，應一併列明欄名及每欄所需內容。摘要可用「五個簡短要點」，程序可用「編號步驟」，工作紙可用「清晰標題、編號題目及獨立教師答案」。

**For reliable reuse:** keep table cells short, separate questions from answers, and do not ask the model to invent rows just to reach a number. A good-looking table is not proof of correctness. If a structure matters, show a tiny example with placeholders clearly labelled as a format illustration, not source evidence. A layout request does not create an editable spreadsheet/PPTX or automatically control Studio. [14]

Google’s chat Help says **Save to note** preserves the original format, including tables and clickable citations. This is documented guidance, not a newly tested school-account operation. Check citations in Notebook before copying; they may not remain usable outside it. [4]

**Optional reuse tip:** Markdown is a plain-text formatting convention, useful if another app specifically needs it; it is not a prerequisite for a table or an accuracy improvement. If you deliberately request raw Markdown for **Google Docs**, use **Tools → Preferences → Enable Markdown**, then **right-click → Paste from Markdown**. Ordinary paste may leave the symbols visible or change the layout. This is a separate Docs step, not a Notebook feature; check the result, particularly tables, equations and citations. We are not assuming any new direct-export function in your Enterprise account. [15]

The ten prompts below now specify their output formats, in both languages and in each worked example. Requesting a format is not a guarantee that the model follows it exactly; use a short reformatting follow-up if needed.

## 1. Check whether our materials cover the HKDSE requirements｜核對教材是否涵蓋文憑試要求

**Playlist connection｜播放清單連繫:** Playlist refinement: identify and isolate sources before cross-referencing them. Adapted from videos 2 and 6. 播放清單啟發：先辨識及聚焦來源，再交叉比較。改編自影片2及6。 [6] [7]

**Use when:** planning an S4–S6 unit or reviewing a subject-panel scheme of work.

**Select:** applicable curriculum guide, applicable assessment framework, and your unit notes or scheme of work.

**English version｜英文版**

```text
I teach HKDSE [subject] to [level]. Compare our [topic] notes with the selected Curriculum and Assessment Guide and assessment framework.

Use a four-column table: Official objective; Evidence in our notes; Coverage (clear / partial / not found); Suggested improvement. Cite both the guidance and our notes. End with three teaching priorities.

Keep curriculum objectives separate from exam rules. Flag anything not stated in the sources. ‘Not found’ refers only to the selected notes.

Write in English.
```

**Traditional Chinese version｜繁體中文版**

```text
我教授HKDSE[科目]，學生為[年級]。請把[課題]筆記與所選《課程及評估指引》及評核大綱比較。

用四欄表格：官方目標；教材證據；涵蓋程度（清楚／部分／未找到）；改進建議。引用官方資料及筆記，最後列出三項備課重點。

區分課程目標與考試規則。資料未有說明的內容，請指出。「未找到」只指所選筆記。

請以繁體中文回答。
```

**Check:** Are the subject and scope correct, and are the selected official documents applicable to your cohort?

### Worked example 4 — Differentiate an S5 Economics explanation｜為中五經濟科設計不同程度的學習支援
