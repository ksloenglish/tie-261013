# Ten bilingual worked prompt examples for Hong Kong teachers

# 香港教師十大提示詞：雙語實例

**How to use｜使用方法:** Copy either the English or the Traditional Chinese version—not both together. Every English example requests “Write in English.” Every Chinese example requests “請以繁體中文回答。” Choose the version matching your desired answer language; original quotations, titles and essential terminology may remain in their source language.

These examples accompany the ten templates in the teacher handout. **They show what to ask, not answers supposedly produced by Notebook.** They have not been tested in the live school account.

**Route A is General subject preparation:** create a notebook with existing notes and worksheets for your teaching subject and one focused topic. These worked examples retain HKDSE cases; for other courses or class levels, replace those fields and omit official-assessment instructions that do not apply. No HKEAA source is required for an ordinary source-based lesson or worksheet, but do not claim an official assessment link without the relevant document.

The subject examples illustrate teacher tasks rather than confirming what is compulsory in a particular HKDSE examination. Upload the applicable official documents before requesting an assessment link. Suggested notebook source names below are labels for documents you supply, not publications that have been created for you.

For HKDSE examples, check that you have permission to use any assessment material. For the EDB examples, import the actual linked PDF from the [Blueprint resource page](https://www.edb.gov.hk/en/edu-system/primary-secondary/applicable-to-primary-secondary/it-in-edu/debp.html). Do not substitute the landing page for the full publication.

## Shorter prompts, with the useful details retained｜精簡而具體的提示詞

The prompts specify **task, audience, output structure and language**. Long source registers and repeated “do not invent” lists have been removed. Related follow-up tasks can be requested after checking the first answer.

**Built in:** Notebook grounds its answers in selected sources and provides citations. **Still useful to specify:** the table columns, which sources to compare, how to show missing evidence, and whether new ideas are suggestions rather than official rules. Grounding is not an accuracy guarantee. [4] [16] [17]

The long warning “Do not invent weightings, marks or compulsory question types” is **not mandatory**. For official-guidance tasks, the shorter “Flag anything not stated in the sources” keeps the important instruction without the long list.

**Reusable, not permanently current:** the examples contain no fixed examination year or year placeholder. Before assessment-related work, select the official documents applicable to your cohort and check their versions. Notebook does not automatically replace old imported documents. [1] [16]

**繁體中文：**提示詞保留任務、對象、格式及語言，刪去重複警告及冗長資料清單。Notebook本身會依據所選來源並提供引用，但不保證完全正確。涉及官方要求時，只需簡短指出「資料未有說明的內容，請指出」。實例不綁定考試年份；使用前仍須選取適用於該屆學生的官方文件及核對版本。

## Output format is part of the example｜輸出格式也是實例的一部分

Each example specifies a useful structure in plain language: point-form briefings, tables with a stated column count and named columns, or numbered worksheet items with a separate teacher key. **You do not need to ask for Markdown.** Column names tell Notebook what information belongs in each column; the number alone does not. Keep citations beside the relevant claims. These are layout requests, not guaranteed exports or live-tested outputs. [14]

每個實例均以淺白指示列明結構：點列摘要、指定欄數及欄名的表格，或編號題目及獨立教師答案。**不必要求Markdown。**只列欄數仍不足，欄名才說明每欄需要甚麼內容；引用應保留在相關聲稱旁。

## Example 1 — Check English reading materials against the requirements｜核對英文閱讀教材的課程及評核涵蓋範圍

**Playlist connection｜播放清單連繫:** Playlist refinement: identify and isolate sources before cross-referencing them. Adapted from videos 2 and 6. 播放清單啟發：先辨識及聚焦來源，再交叉比較。改編自影片2及6。 [6] [7]


**Situation:** Before planning S6 revision, an English teacher wants to know whether the school’s reading-comprehension notes address the skills identified in the selected official guidance.

**Sources to select:** the applicable English Language Curriculum and Assessment Guide; the applicable English Language assessment framework; your teacher-authored notes, labelled **“S6 Reading Revision — teacher notes”**. The notes should contain the actual explanations and practice you want checked, not just a contents page.

**English version｜英文版**

```text
Compare ‘S6 Reading Revision — teacher notes’ with the selected HKDSE English Language Curriculum and Assessment Guide and assessment framework. Focus on reading comprehension and supporting answers with text evidence.

Use a four-column table: Official objective; Evidence in our notes; Coverage (clear / partial / not found); Suggested improvement. Cite both the guidance and our notes. End with three revision-teaching priorities.

Keep curriculum objectives separate from exam rules. Flag anything not stated in the sources. ‘Not found’ refers only to the selected notes.

Write in English.
```

**Traditional Chinese version｜繁體中文版**

```text
請把「S6 Reading Revision — teacher notes」與所選HKDSE英文科《課程及評估指引》及評核大綱比較，集中於閱讀理解及以文本證據支持答案。

用四欄表格：官方目標；教材證據；涵蓋程度（清楚／部分／未找到）；改進建議。引用官方資料及筆記，最後列出三項溫習教學重點。

區分課程目標與考試規則。資料未有說明的內容，請指出。「未找到」只指所選筆記。

請以繁體中文回答。
```

**What a useful result looks like:** a coverage table you can discuss at a panel meeting, with citations beside each claimed objective—not a generic list of reading tips.

**Playlist connection｜播放清單連繫:** Playlist refinement: compare sources and plan one modest visual before Studio generation. Adapted from videos 6 and 12. 播放清單啟發：先比較來源及規劃一張聚焦圖表，再用Studio生成。改編自影片6及12。 [7] [13]


**Situation:** A teacher wants to understand how a broad strategy document relates to a more focused teaching guide, without assuming that either overrides the other.

**Sources to select:** the full Blueprint and the full Supplement II. Give each a distinct source title. Do not use the executive summary as though it were the full Blueprint.

Compare the full ‘Blueprint for Digital Education Development in Primary and Secondary Schools’ with ‘Supplement II: Guide to Using AI in Teaching in Primary and Secondary Schools’. Focus on Hong Kong secondary teachers’ preparation, professional development and checking AI materials.

Use a five-column table: Issue; Blueprint statement and citation; Supplement statement and citation; Relationship/uncertainty; Planning implication. Distinguish scope differences, explicit updates and genuine conflicts. Silence is not a contradiction; do not assume one replaces the other.
